IDIOLECT
Checking Codex

Local style compiler

Turn your writing into a Codex skill.

Idiolect finds choices that recur across your work, checks them against held-out text, then builds a skill you review before installing.

Your samples are evidence, not instructions. This local app keeps raw text out of the generated skill and does not save it. Selected excerpts are sent to OpenAI for two Codex analysis passes under your ChatGPT workspace's data controls.
